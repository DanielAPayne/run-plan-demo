# Drill: the email service goes down

**Date:** 2026-11-11, 07:20 to 07:26 (America/Chicago).
**Question:** if the email service stops, is the lead kept, is the operator told at once, and does the reply go out when the service is back?
**Method:** stop the `mailpit` container, send one lead through the real form entry, wait, start the container again. Times are from the database and the alert channel.

| Time | What happened | Where it is recorded |
|---|---|---|

| 07:25:34 | **The fix:** the email service is started again | drill log |

| 07:25:41 | Both incidents closed with what happened | `incidents` |

## Result

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Was the lead kept? | Yes. It was saved before the reply was tried |
| Was the operator told? | Yes, 0.2 s after the lead, through a channel the outage did not touch |
| Did repeats flood the alerts? | No. Two alerts for two failing steps; the repeat was counted, not re-sent |
| Did the reply go out? | Yes, 2 s after the service returned, 4 min 38 s after the lead |
| Was the lead emailed twice? | No. One message to the lead |

## What the drill did not test

- The service failing after the email was accepted but before it was recorded. That path can send a reply twice.
- A slow or half-working email service. Only a clean stop was tried.
- The watchdog's own checks. They are separate from this path.
